# Disclaimer — ReOpSy

**Last updated: [[DATE]]**

## Summaries are machine-generated

Every summary in ReOpSy is produced automatically. The method is **extractive**:
sentences are selected from the paper's own abstract by a scoring function and
reassembled in their original order. Nothing is rewritten, paraphrased or
invented, and no language model generates prose. This is a deliberate design
choice — an abstractive summariser could state something the authors never
claimed.

Extraction is still lossy. A summary can omit a limitation, a caveat, a
population restriction or a negative result that changes what the paper means.
**Do not cite, rely on, or make a decision from a ReOpSy summary. Read the
paper.**

Summaries are labelled **AI-condensed** on the card and again on the detail
screen, in line with the disclosure requirements introduced by the February 2026
amendment to the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital
Media Ethics Code) Rules.

## Not professional advice

ReOpSy surfaces scientific literature, including biomedical literature. Nothing
in the app is medical, clinical, legal, financial or professional advice. It is
not a diagnostic tool, not a clinical decision-support system, and not regulated
as a medical device. If you are making a decision about your health, consult a
qualified clinician — not a summary card.

## Metadata may be wrong

Titles, authors, years, venues, DOIs and licences come from OpenAlex and arXiv.
These are excellent sources and they still contain errors: mis-parsed author
lists, missing venues, duplicated records, retracted papers that have not been
flagged upstream. ReOpSy passes through what it receives. If a record is wrong,
the correction has to happen at the source; we can remove a listing from our
copy on request.

## We do not host papers

ReOpSy stores bibliographic metadata and machine-generated summaries. It does
not store, host or distribute full texts. Every card links to the publisher,
the repository, or a scholarly search for the title.

## Abstracts and copyright

A full abstract is displayed **only** when the source record declares a licence
that permits reproduction — CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA or CC BY-ND. In every other
case the app shows only its own generated summary and a link out. This rule is
enforced in three independent places: the database column that decides whether
an abstract may be stored, the network layer that reads it, and the component
that renders the card.

If you hold rights in a work and believe this treatment is incorrect, see the
[takedown policy](takedown-policy).

## Retracted and disputed work

The app does not currently flag retractions. A paper appearing in ReOpSy is not
an endorsement of its findings, its methods or its authors. Check the
publisher's record before relying on anything.
