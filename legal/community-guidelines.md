# Community Guidelines — ReOpSy

**Last updated: [[DATE]]**

ReOpSy is a place to read and discuss research carefully. These rules apply to profiles, comments, circles, posts,
replies and recommendations. You accept them when you create a profile.

1. **Be respectful.** Critique the work, not the person. No harassment, threats, hate, or personal attacks.
2. **Be specific and cite.** Point to sections, figures, numbers or other papers. Link sources for factual claims.
3. **No plagiarism or copyright infringement.** Do not post other people's text, figures or full papers. Link to them.
4. **No spam.** Share your own work when it is relevant to the discussion, not everywhere. No ads, no referral links,
   no repeated identical posts.
5. **No medical, legal or financial advice.** Discussing health research is about the research, never about anyone's
   treatment.
6. **Mark uncertainty.** Say when something is a preprint, a hunch, or outside your field.
7. **Respect privacy and confidentiality.** No personal data about others, and no unpublished data, manuscripts or
   peer reviews you hold in confidence.
8. **No illegal content**, including content that violates Indian law or the law where you live.
9. **One account per person. No impersonation.** Your name and affiliation must be your own.

## Enforcement

- Anyone can **Report** a post, comment, profile or circle, and **Block** another member.
- Moderators review reports. We remove content that breaks these rules and may warn, suspend or remove accounts.
  Serious violations (threats, illegal content) lead to immediate removal and, where required, reporting to authorities.
- You can appeal a decision by writing to the grievance officer.
